# Compliance Assumptions — Summary

This is a short pointer. The full documentation of KUSANYA's regulatory
boundary lives in
[compliance/REGULATORY_ASSUMPTIONS.md](compliance/REGULATORY_ASSUMPTIONS.md)
— read that document in full before making any claim to a tenant,
partner, or regulator about what KUSANYA is licensed or permitted to do.

## The one-sentence version

KUSANYA is software that orchestrates billing, control numbers, and
payment collection **through** licensed payment providers/partners — it
is not, and does not claim to be, a bank, payment service provider,
mobile money operator, money transmitter, custodian, or escrow provider,
and it possesses no licenses of any kind. Every regulatory-sounding claim
anywhere in this codebase or its documentation must be read as an
*assumption requiring professional/legal confirmation*, never as an
established fact.

See [compliance/REGULATORY_ASSUMPTIONS.md](compliance/REGULATORY_ASSUMPTIONS.md)
for the full list of assumptions, what the architecture does to keep them
true, and what must be confirmed by qualified legal/compliance counsel
before any of this goes into production with real institutions and real
money.
